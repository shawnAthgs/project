Know the Warning Signs

Learn how common scams work and how to protect yourself.

🎣

Phishing Scam

Fake emails or messages try to trick you into revealing passwords or personal information.

Subject: Urgent Account Verification Required We detected suspicious activity on your bank account. Click the link and enter your account number and password to verify your account or it will be suspended.

How to spot phishing

  • Check the sender's address.
  • Be careful with unexpected links.
  • Watch for urgent threats and pressure.
🧾

Fake Check Scam

A scammer sends a fake check and asks you to send some money back. The check may later bounce, leaving you responsible.

Warning signs

  • Someone sends you money and asks for some back.
  • You must pay fees or buy gift cards.
  • An unexpected job or prize involves depositing a check.
🚨

Emergency Scam

A scammer pretends to be a friend or relative in trouble and urgently asks for money.

How to protect yourself

  • Pause before responding.
  • Contact the person using a number you already know.
  • Verify the story with someone you trust.
  • Don't send money based only on an urgent message.
💔

Romance Scam

A person builds an online relationship to gain trust, then asks for money or private information.

"I've been searching for someone like you for so long. I feel such a deep connection already."

Warning signs

  • Fast, intense declarations of love.
  • Excuses for never meeting or video calling.
  • Requests for money, gift cards, or cryptocurrency.
🔒

Ransomware Scam

Malicious software can lock or encrypt your files. Attackers may demand payment to restore access.

How to stay safer

  • Keep your device and software updated.
  • Avoid suspicious downloads and attachments.
  • Back up important files.
  • Ask a trusted adult or IT professional for help if affected.

Not sure if it's a scam?

Stop before clicking, paying, or sharing information. Verify the message through an official website or a trusted contact.
