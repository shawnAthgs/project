Been Scammed? Here's What to Do

  1. Stop communicating with the suspected scammer. Don't send more money.
  2. Save relevant messages, usernames, transaction details, and screenshots.
  3. Change affected passwords using the official website or app, and enable multifactor authentication.
  4. Contact your bank or payment provider promptly if money or payment details were involved.
  5. Tell a trusted adult if you're a teen, and report the incident to the appropriate organization.

Be cautious of anyone who promises to recover lost money for an upfront fee. Recovery scams often target previous victims.
