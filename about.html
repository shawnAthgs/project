Understanding Online Scams

Learn why online safety matters and how awareness makes a difference.

🎯 Our Mission

CyberHelp was created to educate people—especially teens—about common online scams. Many scams target social media users, gamers, and students. This site helps people recognize warning signs, avoid suspicious offers, and find support when something goes wrong.

📊 Cybercrime Statistics

Online fraud and cybercrime affect people around the world. Official reports help show the scale of the problem, although reported cases do not represent every incident.

  • Phishing is a common way attackers steal account information.
  • Social media and gaming accounts can be targets for fraud.
  • Some victims never report scams, so the full impact is difficult to measure.

Explore current reports from the FBI's IC3 and the FTC's data visualizations.

📱 Why Teens Are Targeted

Teens use social media, messaging apps, online games, and digital marketplaces. Scammers may exploit curiosity, trust, fear of missing out, or the excitement of getting free rewards.

  • Fake giveaways and free in-game currency.
  • Messages pretending to come from friends.
  • Fake login pages and requests for verification codes.

⚠️ Why This Matters

Scams can lead to stolen accounts, exposed personal information, financial loss, or emotional stress. Learning the warning signs helps you pause and check before responding.

  • Protects your personal information and accounts.
  • Helps reduce the risk of financial loss and identity theft.
  • Builds awareness of online manipulation tactics.

🛡️ How to Stay Safe

  • Never share passwords or one-time login codes.
  • Avoid suspicious links and unexpected attachments.
  • Enable two-factor authentication when available.
  • Check website addresses before signing in.
  • Verify unusual requests through another trusted channel.
  • Ask a trusted adult for help if a message feels suspicious.
